Academic Profiles

Profile

Dr. Subhrendu Chattopadhyay is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Science and Engineering domain at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), Patiala. His academic and research background spans computer networks, software-defined networking, distributed systems and performance-oriented networked systems.

He completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati (IITG), with the thesis titled “SDN for Large Scale IoT Networks”, under the supervision of Prof. Sukumar Nandi and mentorship of Dr. Sandip Chakraborty. He received his M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Guwahati in 2014.

Before joining TIET, he served as an Assistant Professor at the Institute for Development and Research in Banking Technology (IDRBT), Hyderabad, where he worked as a co-investigator of the 5G Use Cases Lab for Banking and Financial Services Industries (BFSi) and coordinated the Network Innovation Lab (NIL@IDRBT). He has also held an Assistant Professor position at SRM University, Andhra Pradesh, and worked as a temporary project staff member at IIT Kharagpur.

His current interests include debugging and observability frameworks for Linux kernels and distributed applications, eBPF-based systems monitoring, software-defined networking, distributed algorithms, serverless and edge systems, low-cost quantum key distribution experimentation, and wireless sensing.
